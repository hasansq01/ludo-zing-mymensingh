## Full description (max 4000)
Pull up a chair at a cozy cafe table and play Ludo and Snakes & Ladders in 3D.

Ludo Zing brings the classic board games you grew up with to your phone. Roll the dice, bring your pawns out, capture rivals and race home. Play with friends, with people online, or on your own against the computer.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Online with friends: create a private room, share the six-letter room code and play together.
- Quick match: tap once and get seated with other players online. Pick a table that suits you, from a free Practice table to higher coin tables.
- Vs Computer: play offline against up to three computer opponents. No internet needed.
- Pass & Play: pass one phone around the table with 2 to 4 friends.

TWO CLASSIC GAMES
- Ludo for 2 to 4 players, with Pair rules: land two pawns together to block single opponents. Play on after the first finish to decide 2nd, 3rd and 4th place.
- Snakes & Ladders on a colourful painted board. Climb the ladders, watch out for hungry snakes and race to square 100.

A CAFE FOR EVERY MOOD
- Play in several cafe rooms and Snakes & Ladders scenes, from a cozy cafe to a jungle clearing and a frosty wonderland.
- Switch between the 3D view and a Top View of the board.
- Choose your dice style, avatar, frame, nameplate and badge to make the table yours.

EMOTES
Cheer, laugh, tease or show off with quick emotes during a match, offline or online.

DAILY REWARDS
Come back every day for a daily reward calendar, a free spin and free coins. Level up as you play and unlock more tables and items.

COINS AND GEMS
Coins and gems are virtual items for use inside the game, for example to enter coin tables and to unlock dice, rooms, scenes, avatars and emote packs. They have no real-world value and cannot be cashed out or exchanged for money or prizes. Ludo Zing is a game for fun only. Some items can be bought with gems, and gems can optionally be bought in the game. The game may show optional rewarded videos in exchange for in-game extras.

FRIENDLY AND FAIR
Online games are refereed by our server, so every roll and every move follows the rules. If a friend drops out, a computer player can keep the seat warm until they are back.

Play Ludo with your friends, whenever and wherever you like. Download Ludo Zing and roll the dice.
