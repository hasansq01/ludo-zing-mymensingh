How to play

  1. Get all four of your tokens around the board and into the center.
  2. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your corner.
  3. Land on an opponent to send it back home.
  4. Star squares and colored start squares are safe from capture.
  5. A 6, a capture, or getting a token home earns another roll.
  6. Three 6s in a row and you lose the turn.
  7. You need the exact number to reach the center.
  8. Land a token on another of your own and you can pair them. A pair moves half the roll on even numbers only.
  9. Opponents can't jump a pair. They must land on it, then roll a 1 to pass. Only a pair can capture a pair, and a pair never captures a single token.
  10. A pair splits by itself when it lands on any colored start square or enters its home lane. Pairs can't form there.
