# Audio credits

All the recorded sounds come from **Kenney** (Kenney Vleugels, https://www.kenney.nl) and are released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/: free for personal and
commercial use, no attribution required (we credit Kenney anyway). Each pack's `License.txt` is kept next to the
source files in `godot/tools/audio_src/kenney/<pack>/`.

The synthesised parts (felt marimba, electric piano and nylon-pluck stings, the soft coin bell, the whoosh, the
slide and the music loop) are original to this project, generated by `godot/tools/gen_audio.gd`; they carry no
third-party rights.

Every file below is built by `godot/tools/mix_audio.py`: trimmed, layered, warmed (low-passed; the dice and wood
recordings also get a 4 kHz dip and rounded transients), a little dark room reverb, and set to a quiet level with a
plain gain (no limiter): peaks at or below -6 dBFS, UI taps and steps -16 to -20 dBFS, music -23 LUFS.

## Emote sounds (assets/audio/emotes/)

The 24 emote sounds are built by `godot/tools/make_emote_sfx.py` (reuses the helpers of mix_audio.py). Calm voicing as
above: soft attacks, low-passed at 5.5 kHz, a small dark room, no limiter; active RMS matched to the tap / step sounds
(about -29 dBFS) and peaks at or below the step peak (-17 dBFS). 44.1 kHz mono Ogg Vorbis. Measured levels:
`emotes/levels.json`. Tones, glides, whooshes and the clap are synthesized in numpy (original, no third-party rights).
